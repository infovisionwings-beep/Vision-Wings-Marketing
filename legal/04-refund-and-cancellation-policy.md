# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Vision Wings Marketing**  
Effective Date: **03 August 2026** | Last Updated: **03 August 2026**

---

## 1. Scope

This Policy explains when payments made to **Vision Wings Marketing**, operating as Vision Wings Marketing, can be cancelled or refunded. It applies to all Services purchased through **www.visionwingsmarketing.com** or under a signed proposal, statement of work or retainer agreement ("Engagement Agreement").

Where an Engagement Agreement sets different terms, **the Engagement Agreement prevails**.

## 2. The Principle

We sell professional services delivered by senior practitioners, not stocked goods. Fees compensate reserved capacity and work already performed. **Refunds are therefore assessed on the basis of work delivered and time reserved, not on whether you are satisfied with campaign performance**, which depends on factors outside our control (see our **[Disclaimer](/disclaimer)**).

## 3. Advance Payments and Retainers

- Project engagements typically require an advance of **50%** before work begins. This advance secures your slot in our delivery schedule and is **non-refundable** once discovery, research or design work has commenced.
- Monthly retainers are billed in advance for the coming month and cover reserved capacity for that month.
- Media spend, platform credits, stock licences, fonts, domains, hosting and other third-party costs are **pass-through** and are **non-refundable** once paid to the third party, whether or not the campaign runs to completion.

## 4. Cancellation by You

### 4.1 Before work begins

Written notice received **before** any discovery call, research, strategy or design work has started:

- **Within 48 hours of payment:** full refund of the advance, less payment gateway charges actually incurred.
- **After 48 hours but before commencement:** refund of **75%** of the advance; **25%** retained as a scheduling and administration fee.

### 4.2 After work has begun (project engagements)

You may cancel at any time with written notice. On cancellation we will:

1. Invoice for all work completed to date, valued at the milestone stage reached or, where no milestones are defined, on a pro-rata basis against the agreed scope.
2. Invoice for any third-party costs already committed.
3. Refund the balance of the advance, if any, within **15 working days**.
4. Hand over all deliverables that have been completed **and paid for in full**. Work in progress, source files and unused concepts are not released.

If work completed exceeds the advance held, the difference is payable by you.

### 4.3 Monthly retainers

- **Notice period:** **30 days'** written notice, as specified in the Engagement Agreement.
- The current billing month is **not refundable**; we continue delivering for the remainder of that month.
- Retainers cancelled mid-month do not generate a pro-rata refund, because capacity for that month has already been reserved and cannot be resold.
- Unused hours or deliverables in a retainer month **do not carry forward** unless the Engagement Agreement expressly provides for rollover.

### 4.4 Cancellation due to client inaction

If you do not provide required approvals, assets or platform access for **30 consecutive days** despite two written reminders, we may treat the engagement as cancelled by you, invoice for work completed, and release the reserved capacity.

## 5. Cancellation by Us

We may cancel an engagement where:

- Payment remains overdue beyond **15 days**;
- You are in material breach of the **[Terms of Service](/terms)** or **[Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use)**;
- You require content that is misleading, unlawful, or in breach of a platform's policies;
- Continued collaboration has become unworkable, including through abusive conduct toward our team.

Where we cancel for reasons **other than your breach or non-payment**, we will refund fees for work not yet performed within **15 working days**. Where we cancel because of your breach or non-payment, fees already paid are not refunded and outstanding amounts remain due.

## 6. When Refunds Are Not Available

Refunds will not be issued for:

- Campaign performance falling short of expectations, targets or benchmarks — we do not guarantee results;
- Ad spend already delivered to a platform, or platform costs already incurred;
- Deliverables that have been approved, published, delivered or used;
- Delays caused by your late feedback, missing assets, or withheld access;
- Third-party outages, algorithm changes, ad account suspensions or policy changes;
- Change of business direction, budget reallocation, or internal restructuring on your side, beyond the terms in Clause 4;
- Requests made after **30 days** from the date of the relevant invoice.

## 7. Revisions Before Refunds

Where the concern is quality rather than circumstance, the Engagement Agreement's included revision rounds apply first. Raise a written concern within **7 days** of delivery describing what does not match the agreed brief, and we will revise at no charge within the agreed scope. Most disputes resolve at this stage.

## 8. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

Send a written request to **info.visionwings@gmail.com** with:

1. Registered name and business name;
2. Invoice number and payment date;
3. The engagement or retainer month concerned;
4. The reason for the request and any supporting detail.

**Our process:**

| Stage | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | Within **2 working days** |
| Review and written decision | Within **7 working days** |
| Refund processed, if approved | Within **15 working days** of the decision |
| Credit reflected in your account | A further 5–10 working days, depending on your bank |

Refunds are made to the **original payment method and source account** only. Payment gateway fees, currency conversion charges and bank transfer costs are non-refundable and will be deducted. GST already remitted is adjusted in accordance with GST rules through a credit note.

## 9. Chargebacks

Please raise concerns with us before initiating a chargeback. Chargebacks filed without first following Clause 8 will be contested with documentation of work delivered, and may result in immediate suspension of all Services and recovery of costs.

## 10. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Policy limits any right you have under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** or other applicable Indian law. Where this Policy conflicts with a mandatory statutory right, the statutory right prevails.
