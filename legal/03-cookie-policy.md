## 1. What This Policy Covers

This Cookie Policy explains how **www.visionwingsmarketing.com** (the "Website") uses cookies and similar technologies, what they do, and how you can control them. It should be read alongside our **[Privacy Policy](/privacy)**.

## 2. What Cookies Are

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a website. It allows the site to recognise your device on return visits, remember preferences, and understand how the site is used.

We also use related technologies:

- **Local storage and session storage** — browser-based storage for preferences and session state.
- **Pixels and tracking tags** — small pieces of code from advertising and analytics platforms that record page views and events.
- **Server logs** — records of requests to our servers, including IP address and user agent.

References to "cookies" in this Policy include all of the above.

## 3. Categories of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies *(always active)*

Required for the Website to function. These cannot be switched off through our consent banner.

| Purpose | Examples | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| Session management and login authentication | session token, auth cookie | Session to 30 days |
| Security, CSRF protection, bot mitigation | security tokens | Session |
| Load balancing and routing | hosting/CDN cookies | Session |
| Recording your cookie consent choices | consent preference cookie | 6–12 months |

### 3.2 Functional Cookies *(consent required)*

Remember choices you make to improve your experience — language, theme, dismissed notices, form progress, and embedded media preferences. Disabling these means some features will not remember your settings.

### 3.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies *(consent required)*

Help us understand traffic sources, popular pages, scroll depth and drop-off points so we can improve the Website.

| Provider | Purpose | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| Google Analytics 4 (`_ga`, `_ga_*`) | Distinguishes users, aggregates usage statistics | Up to 24 months |
| Vercel Analytics | Page performance and traffic measurement | Up to 24 months |

Data is aggregated and used to measure site performance, not to identify you personally.

### 3.4 Advertising and Retargeting Cookies *(consent required)*

Used to measure campaign effectiveness and to show you relevant advertising on other platforms.

| Provider | Purpose | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| Google Ads / Google Tag (`_gcl_*`) | Conversion tracking, remarketing | Up to 90 days |
| Meta Pixel (`_fbp`) | Conversion tracking, custom audiences | Up to 90 days |
| LinkedIn Insight Tag | B2B conversion tracking, audience matching | Up to 180 days |

### 3.5 Embedded Third-Party Content

Pages containing embedded video, maps or social feeds may set cookies controlled by the embedding provider (for example, YouTube, Vimeo, Google Maps). We do not control those cookies; they are governed by the provider's own policy.

## 4. Legal Basis and Consent

Strictly necessary cookies are set on the basis of legitimate use. **All other cookies are set only after you give consent** through the cookie banner presented on your first visit.

Under the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023**, consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, and must be as easy to withdraw as to give. Our banner therefore offers **"Accept all", "Reject all" and "Manage preferences"** with equal prominence, and rejection does not restrict access to the Website.

## 5. How to Manage Cookies

**On this Website:** click **Cookie Settings** in the footer at any time to change or withdraw your consent. Changes apply from the moment they are saved; cookies already set may need to be cleared in your browser.

**In your browser:** most browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies through their settings menu —

- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
- **Safari:** Settings → Privacy
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security
- **Edge:** Settings → Cookies and site permissions

**Platform-level opt-outs:**

- Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on: `tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout`
- Google Ads settings: `myadcenter.google.com`
- Meta ad preferences: through your Facebook or Instagram account settings

Blocking all cookies, including strictly necessary ones, may prevent login and break parts of the Website.

## 6. Do Not Track

Browsers vary in how they implement "Do Not Track" signals and there is no agreed standard for responding to them. We currently do not respond to DNT headers, and rely on the consent mechanism described above instead. Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals, where received, are treated as a rejection of non-essential cookies.
